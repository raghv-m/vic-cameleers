# Scorecard (Dieter Rams)

1. Good design is innovative — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: default shadcn card grids, Oswald + Inter, no images (01 Visual).
   Justification: imitates the generic local-business template; the cameleer story exists only in text.
2. Good design makes a product useful — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: phone + quote in header and sticky bar; calculator sits 4th section down; quote is 5 steps.
   Justification: the task completes, but the price tool is below the fold and the form has extra steps.
3. Good design is aesthetic — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: blank navy band and fleet cards in screenshots; soft rounded cards beside a hard navy band.
   Justification: one jarring violation (invisible sections) plus several inconsistencies.
4. Good design makes a product understandable — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: clear CTAs and nav; 12 sections dilute where to look.
   Justification: controls are clear, but structure repeats itself so the order is hard to follow.
5. Good design is unobtrusive — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: chrome is quiet; decorative camel watermark in hero.
   Justification: chrome recedes, but empty decoration competes with content.
6. Good design is honest — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: no invented proof; "no surprises" repeated, "Professional" unbacked (01 Copy).
   Justification: claims are true but one inflation and repetition blunt them.
7. Good design is long-lasting — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: rounded cards with hover lift, numbered badges, trend font pairing.
   Justification: 2-3 dated template markers.
8. Good design is thorough — Score: 1/3
   Evidence: CTA contrast 4.41:1, 3.62:1 on final CTA, no error.tsx, no aria-describedby.
   Justification: two to three states or details missing or failing.
9. Good design is environmentally friendly — Score: 2/3
   Evidence: 271 KB JS, reduced motion honoured, no idle animation.
   Justification: under 500 KB with motion gated, but well over 100 KB.
10. Good design is as little design as possible — Score: 1/3
    Evidence: process told twice, differentiators three times (01 Structural).
    Justification: 3-5 removable, duplicated sections.

**Total: 15/30**
